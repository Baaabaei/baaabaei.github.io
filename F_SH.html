About

I recently completed my PhD at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU Wind and Energy Systems), where I developed and validated EMT models of hybrid renewable power plants and studied how they behave on weak grids. Before that I worked for six years as an electrical engineer on industrial generation systems, and as a power systems researcher on converter control.

I am looking for a long-term engineering role in wind, hybrid power plants or grid integration in Denmark.

What I work on

  • Hybrid power plants on weak gridsPlant-level control that keeps voltage and frequency stable when wind, solar and storage share one grid connection.
  • Grid-forming and grid-following controlComparing converter control strategies and their limits under low short-circuit strength.
  • EMT modelling and compliancePSCAD models parameterised, benchmarked and checked against grid codes (ENTSO-E, Energinet) and OEM model behaviour under faults.
